# Team: Team Torque, Vasavi College of Engineering Vehicle: 24172 | Competition: ATVC 2024 | Result: 3rd Place, Design Evaluation (19th overall / 120 teams)

## TL;DR

Served as Vice Captain and Project Manager of the team, contributing to the design, fabrication, and testing of a full off-road all-terrain vehicle for the SAE ATVC 2024 competition. The team converted from **2WD to a full-time 4WD system**, reduced chassis weight from 27 kg to 25 kg, and shortened the vehicle footprint by over 25 cm while validating all major subsystems through hand calculations, Ansys FEA, and MSC Adams simulations. In addition to steering system development, I coordinated procurement activities, budgeting, sponsorship acquisition, and manufacturing planning, gaining valuable experience in managing both the technical and organizational aspects of a large engineering project. The vehicle secured **3rd place in Design Evaluation out of 120 teams**. Prior to this, the team competed in **BAJA SAEINDIA 2023**, qualifying for and racing at the NATRAX facility in Pithampur.

## Vehicle Overview

The vehicle was developed around a unibody roll-cage architecture with a primary focus on driver safety, reliability, and off-road performance. Rather than iterating on previous designs, every major subsystem was independently researched, designed, analyzed, and validated to achieve measurable improvements in performance, manufacturability, and vehicle dynamics.

As Vice Captain and Project Manager, I was involved in technical development as well as procurement planning, budgeting, sponsor coordination, and manufacturing execution, ensuring successful integration of engineering and project management activities throughout the vehicle development cycle.

### Key Improvements Over Previous Iteration

| Specification | Previous Vehicle | Current Vehicle |
|--------------|----------------|----------------|
| Drive Configuration | 2WD | 4WD |
| Frame Weight | 27 kg | 25.03 kg |
| Overall Length | 2108.2 mm | 1832.35 mm |
| Overall Width | 1489.6 mm | 966.79 mm |
| Steering Mechanism | Ackermann | Anti-Ackermann |
| Steering Ratio | 12:1 | 6.4:1 |
| Top Speed | 53 km/h | 56 km/h |
| Maximum Torque | 520 Nm | 524.20 Nm |
| Gradeability | 64% | 65% |

The transition to a full-time **4WD drivetrain**, adoption of **Anti-Ackermann steering geometry**, reduction in vehicle dimensions and chassis weight, and comprehensive validation through engineering calculations, Ansys FEA, and MSC Adams simulations contributed to a more responsive, compact, and capable off-road platform.

# Project Overview

Team Torque is the official All-Terrain Vehicle team of Vasavi College of Engineering. The objective was to design, manufacture, and validate a competition-ready ATV capable of meeting strict performance, safety, and design requirements under limited budget and time constraints.

As Steering Lead and Project Manager, I was responsible not only for steering system design but also for coordinating cross-functional project activities including procurement, sponsorship acquisition, production planning, stakeholder communication, and manufacturing execution.

This project sparked my interest in industrial systems, manufacturing operations, supply chains, and management engineering.

---

# Problem Statement

Develop a steering system that could:

- Deliver accurate and responsive vehicle handling
- Minimize tire scrub during cornering
- Improve maneuverability in off-road conditions
- Meet competition safety standards
- Be manufactured within project budget and timeline constraints
