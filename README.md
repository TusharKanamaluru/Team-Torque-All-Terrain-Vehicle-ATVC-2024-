Served as Vice Captain and Project Manager of the team, contributing to the design, fabrication, and testing of a full off-road all-terrain vehicle for the SAE ATVC 2024 competition. The vehicle secured **3rd place in Design Evaluation out of 120 teams**. Prior to this, the team competed in **BAJA SAEINDIA 2023**, qualifying for and racing at the NATRAX facility in Pithampur.

# Project Overview

Team Torque is the official All-Terrain Vehicle team of Vasavi College of Engineering. The objective was to design, manufacture, and validate a competition-ready ATV capable of meeting strict performance, safety, and design requirements under limited budget and time constraints.

As Steering Lead and Project Manager, I was responsible not only for steering system design but also for coordinating cross-functional project activities including procurement, sponsorship acquisition, production planning, stakeholder communication, and manufacturing execution.

This project sparked my interest in industrial systems, manufacturing operations, supply chains, and management engineering.

---

# Problem Statement

Develop a steering system that could:

- Deliver accurate and responsive vehicle handling
- Minimize tire scrub during cornering
- Improve maneuverability in off-road conditions
- Meet competition safety standards
- Be manufactured within project budget and timeline constraints
